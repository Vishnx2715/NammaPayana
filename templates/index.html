The Architecture Difference

Why a Deterministic Reality Engine?

Travel planning requires physical feasibility, not imaginative chat text.

✕ Generic AI Chatbot Planners

LLMs generating itineraries without grounding in verified data:

  • ✕Hallucinates Prices: Invents hotel tariffs and monument entry fees with high confidence.
  • ✕Impossible Schedules: Plans 6 attractions across 400 km in a single afternoon.
  • ✕Silent Over-Budget: Exceeds your stated budget without a single warning.
  • ✕Flat-Earth Routes: Ignores Western Ghats mountain topography and real road detour factors.

✓ Namma Payana Reality Engine

Pure Python deterministic validation — checks its own output at every step:

  • ✓Verified Database: Only destinations from data/destinations.json are permitted — no invented places.
  • ✓Schedule & Pacing: Relaxed ≤7h, Balanced ≤9h, Packed ≤11h active window enforced per day.
  • ✓Exact Decimal Arithmetic: Over-budget is flagged with the exact rupee gap and an automated fix suggestion.
  • ✓Ghats Road Factor: 1.3× road detour buffer applied to every intercity leg through Karnataka.
